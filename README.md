# Nexora

Nexora is an evolving business discovery, digital presence analysis, and workflow automation project. Its long-term goal is to support a connected process from identifying opportunities to qualifying prospects, preparing solutions, and tracking outcomes.

> **Project status:** Under active development. Planned capabilities should not be interpreted as completed features.

## Vision

Build a modular, secure platform for discovering business opportunities, assessing digital presence, organizing prospects, and supporting human-reviewed AI-assisted workflows.
